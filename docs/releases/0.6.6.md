Qard 0.6.6 adds statistics for a single deck, so you can see how each part of a course is going.

- **Deck statistics:** choose the chart button beside **Study** on a deck's page, or a deck's name under **Your decks** on the Statistics page. Reviews, recall, study activity, ratings, upcoming reviews and memory estimates then cover only that deck and its subdecks.
- **Compare sections of a course:** the table compares a deck's subdecks (such as `ASD`, `FP` and `TOA` inside a course deck), then the topics of its own cards, with cards studied, recall and cards due for each. Open a subdeck to see its statistics by topic, or study any row directly.
- **History follows your cards:** reviews are matched by card, so topics moved into a subdeck keep the statistics they had before the move.

Card IDs, review history and file formats are unchanged.

All 767 automated tests, formatting, type checking, source and stylesheet lint, build and release checks pass locally.

To install manually, download `main.js`, `manifest.json` and `styles.css` from this release and replace those three files in your vault's `.obsidian/plugins/qard/` folder. Reload Qard to apply the update. Keep `data.json`, the `study-backups` folder and your vault notes to preserve settings and study progress.
