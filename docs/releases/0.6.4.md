Qard 0.6.4 fixes adding suggested cards from a lesson, and gives the study card a lifted look.

- **Lesson cards respect a note's fixed topic:** adding a suggested card from a lesson recap could fail when the lesson's title differed from the source note's `qard-topic`. Lesson cards now go to that fixed topic, and new cards for a different topic are written to a sibling note instead of the restricted one. ([#12](https://github.com/kylianmarceau/qard-obsidian/pull/12))
- **Lifted study card:** the card you are studying sits one shade off the page with a soft shadow, in both light and dark themes, so it stands out from the pane around it.

Card IDs, review history and file formats are unchanged.

All 756 automated tests, formatting, type checking, source and stylesheet lint, build and release checks pass locally.

To install manually, download `main.js`, `manifest.json` and `styles.css` from this release and replace those three files in your vault's `.obsidian/plugins/qard/` folder. Reload Qard to apply the update. Keep `data.json`, the `study-backups` folder and your vault notes to preserve settings and study progress.
